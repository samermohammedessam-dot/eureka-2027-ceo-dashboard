- **Discovery → mission bridge:** existing gold geometry → blueprint → hospital, compressed and re-cut onto the music.
- **Services:** the unrelated background photos are removed. Each service becomes a two-panel cinematic composition of relevant actor footage, shown full-height and never enlarged, next to its title.
